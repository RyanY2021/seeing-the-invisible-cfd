# Making of v2 — "Seeing the Invisible" (with Clawd)

v2 retells the v1 film at a gentler pace (4:46 instead of 2:35) in a neon / synthwave / acrylic style,
with Clawd — the little orange pixel critter of Claude Code — as the curious guide who asks the questions.
Everything is still generated by code: simulations (NVIDIA Warp on one GPU), Blender scenes built by scripts,
a 2.5D paint shader in numpy, a procedural score, and a code-only compositor.

## What is real simulation, what is illustration
| Shot | What you see | How it was made |
|---|---|---|
| V01–V03, V25–V26 | incense smoke | **3D lattice-Boltzmann** plume (192×384×192, smoke on a 2× grid), the same VDBs as v1 |
| V02 | Clawd leaps through the plume and the smoke curls around it | **the plume re-simulated with Clawd as a moving solid body** (direct-forcing immersed body, `src/lbm3d_body.py`) |
| V04 | streamlines on the holographic cards | **potential flow** (Laplace equation for the stream function, solved around each shape) |
| V05 | the neon river around the pillar | analytic potential flow past a cylinder |
| V06 | pressure plunger | illustration (beads crowd towards the piston) |
| V07 | sliding plates | **1D momentum diffusion** between the plates (the viscous term, literally) |
| V08 | smoke ring from an air cannon | **3D lattice-Boltzmann**: a short push through a round hole rolls up into a vortex ring |
| V09 | hose jet | kinematics: same droplets per second, half the opening → twice the speed |
| V10–V11 | Navier–Stokes | the equations (matplotlib mathtext) on a lacquer slab; Voronoi shatter |
| V13 | cells around the car | a real **octree mesh** refined towards the car surface (21 k cells) |
| V15–V17 | swirls behind pixel-Clawd | **2D lattice-Boltzmann** flow past the Clawd sprite (2048×768, Re ≈ 220), dye shaded as glossy acrylic paint; the same run at 128×48 and 512×192 for the resolution shot |
| V16 | clouds behind an island | Landsat 7 photo, NASA/GSFC (public domain) |
| V18–V19 | turbulent billows; RANS/LES/DNS | the v1 **Kelvin–Helmholtz LES** (3840×2160); LES = spatial filter, RANS = time + streamwise average |
| V20 | wing-tip vortices | **vortex-sheet roll-up** (Krasny method, 3 M smoke markers) behind an elliptic wing |
| V21 | car in the wind tunnel | **3D lattice-Boltzmann** with smoke streaklines (v1 data) |
| V22 | offshore wind farm | scene only (v1 ocean + turbines, synthwave sky) |
| V23 | winds over the globe | **real NOAA GFS 10 m winds** of 12–13 Oct 2026, particles advected on the GPU |
| V24 | blood through an artery | analytic pulsatile flow in a carotid-like bifurcation (illustrative), red cells advected |

## Look
- Render once in scene-linear EXR, grade in code (`src/grade.py`): bloom in linear light → AgX tone map → a
  slight prismatic RGB fringe → vignette → film grain. A cheap "fake haze" glow replaced real volumetric haze in
  the Cycles incense shots (4× faster, indistinguishable at this level).
- "Car-ad" lighting hides simple models: black lacquer bodies drawn only by long strip-light reflections, while
  light linking keeps the coloured smoke lights off the car.
- 2D flows are not flat colour maps: `src/paint25d.py` turns a dye field into poured acrylic paint — banded
  neon palette, height from the dye, normals, soft heightfield shadows, glossy strip reflections with a
  thin-film hue shift.
- Clawd (`blender/clawd.py`) is built from the Claude Code banner sprite (18×5 pixels of 1:2 aspect) as glossy
  voxels, with pixel-art acting: hops with squash & stretch on the beat, eyes that slide by whole pixels,
  blinks, waves, a voxel burst. Each shot exports Clawd's screen track and action cues; the compositor draws
  the pixel speech bubbles and the sound effects follow the cues.

## Sound
- Score (`src/score/score_v2.py`): built on the Tidepool engine from the companion project
  (droplet plinks, kalimba, babbling brook, gliding liquid chords, droplet hi-hats) — softened (no impacts or heavy
  drops, round clickless kick, snaps instead of big snares), slowed to 120 BPM so one bar = 48 frames and every
  cut lands on the grid; an 8-bit chip voice appears when we enter the computer.
- SFX (`src/sfx_v2.py`): Clawd's 8-bit "voice" and actions, neon buzz, whooshes and glitches on transitions —
  all synthesised and placed from the shots' cue lists.
